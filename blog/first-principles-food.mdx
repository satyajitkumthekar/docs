## There is exactly one principle

**Fat is lost when energy out exceeds energy in.** No fourth possibility. No food is exempt.

Keto works when it puts you in a deficit. Fasting works when it puts you in a deficit. Neither is magic and neither is required. You finally have a filter.

## Protein decides what the loss is made of

Calories decide whether the scale moves. Protein decides whether that movement is fat or muscle. Same ten kilos, two completely different bodies.

## Your two numbers

<Frame>
  ![Your Two Numbers](/images/your-numbers.png "Your Two Numbers")
</Frame>

**Calories.** Get your maintenance from a TDEE calculator, then take off five hundred to lose, leave it to maintain, add three hundred to build.

<Card title="Get your maintenance number" icon="calculator" horizontal href="https://tdeecalculator.net/">
  Thirty seconds. Be honest on the activity level, because that is the input people inflate.
</Card>

**Protein.** Your weight in kilos times 2.2. That number in grams. Eighty kilos is a hundred and seventy five grams a day.

Both are estimates. Run them two weeks, take the weekly average on the scale, and let your own body correct the number.

## The anchor method

This is the one we build with everyone.

It is not better at burning fat. Nothing is, once the number is the same. It is better because it is the only one built for the person instead of the deficit.

And it is not a mix of the others. It falls out of your two numbers. Protein is due daily, so it needs the anchor meal. Calories settle weekly, so they need banking.

<Frame>
  ![The Anchor Method](/images/the-anchor-method.png "The Anchor Method")
</Frame>

### The anchor meal

One high protein meal a day carrying most of the load. Mine is seventy grams, so the rest of the day only has to find a hundred more, which happens almost by accident.

Build two or three versions. One you cook, one you can buy, one that needs nothing. The anchor moves around your day. What does not move is that it happens.

<Frame>
  ![The Anchor Meal](/images/the-anchor.png "The Anchor Meal")
</Frame>

### Calorie banking

Two thousand a day is fourteen thousand a week. A wedding that costs four thousand is sixteen fifty a day across the other six, and the week still lands.

Bank before the event, never after. And do not lend so hard that you turn up starving.

<Frame>
  ![Calorie Banking](/images/calorie-banking.png "Calorie Banking")
</Frame>

### What it hands you

Protein secured, calories allocated, so the shape of your day is free. One meal on a travel day. Two at the office. Five at home. None of it is a rule you owe tomorrow.

<Frame>
  ![The Shape Is Yours](/images/three-shapes.png "The Shape Is Yours")
</Frame>

Read the day instead of obeying a plan. How many windows do I have. Which meal is not mine to control. Is this shape carrying my protein.

## The situations you know are coming

| Situation | What you do |
| :-- | :-- |
| Restaurant | That meal is the anchor. Protein first, build the order around it. |
| Travel day | One real meal and one fallback. Anchor at the airport, not on the plane. |
| Family food | Eat the food. Add your own protein and let the carbs be what they are. |
| No time to cook | Anchor plus fallback. Nothing that needs a free evening. |
| Wedding or big night | Bank the days before. Hold protein, spend everything else. |
| Drinking | Count it in advance. Anchor before you go. |
| Trip to India | Maintenance, not a deficit. Anchor daily, walk daily. |
| A week that fell apart | Do not wait for Monday. Anchor tomorrow and the week is back. |
